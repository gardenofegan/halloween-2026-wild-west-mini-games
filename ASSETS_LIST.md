# Wild West Mini-Games Asset Recommendations

All assets below are recommended from [Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets) (Public Domain / CC0).

## Global Assets
*   **UI / Menus**: **UI Pack (Base)** or **UI Pack (RPG Expansion)** (great for wooden panels, menu buttons, and borders).
*   **SFX**: **Interface Sounds** (for menu navigation) and **Impact Sounds** (for general hits, whacks, and thuds).

## Mini-Game Specific
### 1. Quick Draw
*   **Art**: **Shooter Pack** (contains great revolvers, bullets, and cowboy-style accessories).
*   **Audio**: **Impact Sounds** (for the gunshots).

### 2. Gold Rush
*   **Art**: **Platformer Art: Complete** or **Mining Pack** (has perfect minecarts, rails, and gold nuggets).
*   **Audio**: **Casino Audio** (perfect for the jingling sound of gold collecting).

### 3. Lasso Catch
*   **Art**: **Animal Pack** (for the cows) and **Physics Pack** (for the rope/lasso segments).

### 4. Bandit Whack
*   **Art**: **Sokoban Pack** (has great wooden barrels) and **Shape Characters** (use these to easily color-code the bandits).

### 5. Dynamite Toss
*   **Art**: **Platformer Art** (has dynamite sticks and safes/crates) and **Particle Pack** (for the explosion visuals).
*   **Audio**: **Explosion Audio** (for the "BOOM" and "FIZZLE" sounds).

### 6. Horse Race
*   **Art**: **Animal Pack** (for the horses).
*   **Audio**: **RPG Audio** (often contains great rhythmic footsteps or galloping).

### 7. Snake Bite
*   **Art**: **Animal Pack** (for the snakes).
*   **Audio**: **Interface Sounds** (you can loop a rapid ticking/clicking sound to simulate a rattle).

### 8. Telegraph Decoder
*   **Art**: **UI Pack** (for the glowing telegraph lights).
*   **Audio**: **Digital Audio** (perfect for the electronic DOT and DASH beeps).
