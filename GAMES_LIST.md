# Wild West Mini-Games Collection

Here is the master list and mechanics for the 8 mini-games planned for the photo booth experience. Each game is designed to be played exclusively with the Red Button on the Buzz Controllers, lasting no longer than 60 seconds per round to keep the party moving.

### 1. Quick Draw (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Reaction Time
- **Gameplay:** The screen tells players to "WAIT FOR IT...". Suddenly, "DRAW!" appears on screen. The first player to hit their Red Button wins the round. If a player hits their button before "DRAW!" appears, they get a "FALSE START" and are disqualified for that round.

### 2. Gold Rush (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Stamina / Button Mashing
- **Gameplay:** Players must mash the Red Button as fast as possible to fill their on-screen minecart with gold before time runs out. The player with the most gold at the end wins.

### 3. Lasso Catch (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Precision Timing
- **Gameplay:** A cow (or target) moves back and forth across a target zone on the screen. Players must hit the Red Button to throw their lasso exactly when the cow is lined up in the center.

### 4. Bandit Whack (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Recognition
- **Gameplay:** Bandits pop up on screen wearing specific player colors (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue). When a bandit matching your specific color pops up, you must hit your Red Button to whack them. Hitting the wrong color deducts points.

### 5. Dynamite Toss (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Hold & Release Timing
- **Gameplay:** Players hold down the Red Button to build power on a swinging meter. They must release the button right when the meter hits the "sweet spot" to successfully toss the dynamite and blow up a safe.

### 6. Horse Race (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Rhythm
- **Gameplay:** A heartbeat pulse plays on screen (and via audio if available). Players must press the Red Button exactly on the beat. Hitting it on the beat makes your horse sprint forward; mashing it off-beat makes your horse stumble and slow down.

### 7. Snake Bite (Red Light, Green Light) (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Inhibition
- **Gameplay:** A rattlesnake is on screen. While it shakes its tail and rattles, players must NOT press anything. Suddenly, it stops rattling and strikes—players must press the Red Button instantly! Any false starts eliminate the player from the round.

### 8. Telegraph Decoder (IMPLEMENTED ✅)
- **Mechanic:** Memory
- **Gameplay:** The game flashes a sequence of short and long signals (like Morse code). Players must memorize the sequence and repeat it back using short taps or long holds on the Red Button to crack the safe.
