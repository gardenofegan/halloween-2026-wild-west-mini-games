Wild West Mini-Games: Cheat Sheet

Halloween 2026 Event

1. Quick Draw

Goal: Shoot your rival first!
How: Wait for "DRAW!". Press RED to fire. Don't shoot early!

2. Gold Rush

Goal: Mine the most gold.
How: Mash RED as fast as you can before time runs out!

3. Lasso Catch

Goal: Catch running cows.
How: Press RED when a cow enters the center dashed zone.

4. Bandit Whack

Goal: Whack bandits, avoid barrels.
How: Press RED when a Bandit pops up. Don't hit Barrels!

5. Dynamite Toss

Goal: Blow up the bank vault.
How: Press RED when the sliding meter is in the center GREEN zone.

6. Horse Race

Goal: Jump over the hurdles.
How: Press the matching COLORED BUTTON (Blue, Orange, Green, Yellow) as your horse reaches a hurdle.

7. Snake Bite

Goal: Survive the rattler.
How: Wait for the snake to scream "STRIKE!", then press RED quickly!

8. Telegraph Decoder

Goal: Decode the secret message.
How: Memorize the center light. When it's your turn, repeat the sequence using RED (Tap = DOT, Hold = DASH).

Keep your fingers ready and watch your lane! Good luck!
